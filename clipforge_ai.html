What works in this version

  • Detects YouTube, Twitch, Vimeo, and other URLs.
  • Lets you choose a local video, set timestamps, and export a 9:16 crop where the browser supports the required processing.
  • Generates an SRT subtitle file from your caption text.

Not implemented yet: automatic AI highlight discovery, speech-to-text transcription, face tracking, and direct extraction from third-party video URLs. Those require compatible media access and additional local AI components.

Privacy: your selected local video is processed in your browser; this prototype does not upload it to a ClipForge server. FFmpeg tools are loaded from public CDNs when you press Create, so an internet connection is required for that step. Only process videos you own or have permission to edit.
